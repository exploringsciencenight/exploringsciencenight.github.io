Hands-On & Interactive Gadgets

Station Note: These gadgets are interactive! Supervision helps keep delicate components safe for everyone to enjoy.

Standalone Gadgets

  • Boomerang Wheel: Needs open floor space to roll and curve back.
  • Energy Stick: Demonstrates electrical conductivity through touch.
  • Hand Boiler: Liquid rises from palm warmth (requires careful handling).
  • Magnetic Hourglass: Generates iron filings shapes.
  • Night Vision Goggles: Requires a dimmed room or nighttime area.

Stationary & Table Experiments

  • Newton’s Cradle: Demonstrates conservation of momentum.
  • Plasma Ball: Touch-responsive globe (needs outlet access).
  • Frog Holograms: Optical illusion using 3D parabolic mirrors.
  • Maglev Train: Magnetic levitation shuttle over track rails.
  • Refraction Prisms: Splits white light into spectrum colors.
  • Snap Circuits: Safe modular electronics builder.
  • Microscope: 100-slide tray (volunteers assist with slide handling).
